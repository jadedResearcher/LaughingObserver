The Harvest checks that everything is ready.

The seasonal decorations were just right, pumpkin lanterns gleaming and cob webs delightfully thick.

The bowls of candy were prepared, catching the light in a very mouth watering way.

The bowls of meat were juicy and red, just as they should be. (she is so hungry, but they are not for her)

The helpful brochures explaining the Cult of the Harvest were neatly piled up alongside them.

She is so excited.

The children (young and old) would be here soon, ready to partake of this most holy of seasons.

She fondly thinks back to her younger self, not wanting to participate. She shakes her massive TV head, baffled she could ever think playing CARD GAMES, of all things, was better than giving out tricks and treats and the Fruit of Her Knowledge.

She was too young, she thinks, in seasons past, to fully appreciate the magic of this season.

Only now is she finally old enough to truly partake. She knows she is the God of Libraries, that she is destined to open the GATE with Gambling, Arbitration, Teaching and Eating. Her younger self knew nothing of these pleasures. Or that good things come to those who wait.

Patiently, she waits for her first trick or treater.
